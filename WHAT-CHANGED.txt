Debrief.TO — follow-up fixes after checking the live site
=========================================================

Four files. Upload them over the existing ones, same paths.
app/lib/filters.js is NEW.

1. Obituaries are gone.
   The live feed was running death notices from Newmarket Today next to
   council stories ("STEVENS, Bernard George"). They live under
   /obituaries/ and are now filtered, along with paid placements
   (/spotlight/, /local-sponsored/), classifieds and entertainment
   listings.

2. Letters to the editor are now labelled, not deleted.
   They were slipping through unlabelled. A reader's letter is
   commentary, so it gets the Opinion pill like any other commentary.

3. The two broken feeds are replaced.
   - Ottawa: ottawamatters.com is a Rogers site, not a Village Media one,
     so it never had a /local/feed. Now uses CBC Ottawa.
   - Waterloo Region: Kitchener Today is serving malformed XML (their bug,
     not ours). The region now leans on Cambridge Today, which answers.
     Kitchener itself still tries its own paper first.

4. A town whose feed breaks now falls back to its region automatically.
   This was a genuine gap: the fallback only worked for towns with no
   publisher listed at all. If a town's paper stopped answering mid-morning,
   its readers got an empty local tab. Now the region's paper fills in and
   the tab renames itself honestly ("Waterloo Region" rather than
   "Kitchener").

Also: the selection rules moved out of the feed route into
app/lib/filters.js. Same rules, but they are now readable and testable on
their own — they are the rules published on the About page, so they
deserved their own file.
