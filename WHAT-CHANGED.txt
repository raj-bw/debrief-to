CONTENTS

1. The two mis-tags are fixed. A publisher tagging something "Politics" or
   "Government" no longer makes it National Politics — those words describe
   the subject, not the scale, and scale is what that tab claims. Category
   matching is whole-word now, not substring, which is what put The Breach's
   Doug Ford profile under Environment. Cards show up to two topics.

2. Picking a town selects its chip and filters the feed in one step.

3. Toronto is no longer a hometown option; it already has its own place tab.

4. The About page says who owns whom, generated from the data.

5. /api/verify-feeds — 149 candidate publishers from your research, tested
   against six common feed paths, from Vercel.
