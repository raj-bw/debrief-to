## Design Context

### Users
Toronto residents (primarily young professionals, civically curious 20-35 year olds) who want to stay informed about local news but don't have time to check 11 different publishers. They arrive wanting a quick scan of what matters locally — not a deep research session. They may visit daily or a few times a week.

### Brand Personality
**Trustworthy, modern, approachable.**

Debrief.TO is the friend who reads all the local news and gives you the highlights. Not a megaphone, not a firehose — a calm, reliable briefing. The tone is confident but never loud.

### Emotional Goals
- Users should feel **informed**, not overwhelmed
- The experience should feel **curated**, like someone thoughtful picked these for you
- Avoid overstimulation — no visual bombardment, no competing elements screaming for attention
- Quiet confidence: the design earns trust through restraint, not flash

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone**: Editorial calm. Clean, spacious, confident. Closer to a well-designed magazine than a news aggregator.
- **References**: Axios (scannable brevity), The Browser (curated calm), Apple News (clear card hierarchy), Monocle (sophisticated editorial)
- **Anti-references**: Reddit (chaotic, dense, overwhelming), clickbait news sites, anything with walls of text, flashing elements, or competing visual hierarchies. Nothing that makes your eyes hurt.
- **Theme**: Both light and dark mode. Light mode is warm cream (#FAF8F5), not sterile white. Dark mode is deep charcoal (#1A1A1A), not pure black.

### Design Principles
1. **Breathing room over density** — Generous whitespace. Every element earns its place. When in doubt, remove.
2. **One focal point at a time** — Clear visual hierarchy. The eye should know exactly where to go. No competing calls to action.
3. **Warm, not clinical** — Cream backgrounds, soft shadows, rounded corners, warm greys. Human and approachable, not sterile tech.
4. **Color with purpose** — Publisher brand colors are functional identifiers, not decoration. The green (#2D6A4F) accent is the only brand color that should feel prominent.
5. **Progressive disclosure** — Show the minimum needed. Filters, sources, and details reveal on interaction. Don't front-load complexity.

### Typography
- **Headings**: Georgia serif — editorial authority, warmth, trust
- **Body**: DM Sans — clean, modern, highly legible at small sizes
- **Sizing**: Restrained. Don't shout. Let hierarchy come from weight and spacing, not size jumps.

### Color System
- Primary accent: #2D6A4F (deep green — trust, civic, nature)
- Light mode bg: #FAF8F5 (warm cream)
- Dark mode bg: #1A1A1A (deep charcoal)
- Publisher colors: functional only, used for source identification dots and category tints
- Dark mode glows: publisher colors boosted +160 per channel, used sparingly for button outlines

### Accessibility (planned)
- WCAG AA compliance target for future iteration
- Color blindness considerations to be addressed
- Reduced motion preferences to be respected
